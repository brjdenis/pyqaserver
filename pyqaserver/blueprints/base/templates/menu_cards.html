Security

Be aware that pyQAserver does not implement top-notch security features. Therefore, follow these rules to make things safe:

  • Do not upload patient data in any form. Use only identity-free data that results from your QA procedures.
  • Do not serve the app on a public server. Run the app on a local computer connected to your local network.
  • The database is not protected with a password, so keep the computer where it is located under lock and key.
